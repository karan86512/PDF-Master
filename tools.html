Welcome to PDFMaster User Guide

PDFMaster makes managing your PDF files simple, fast, and secure. This guide will help you understand how to use our PDF tools efficiently. Follow the instructions below to merge, split, compress, convert, edit, secure, and optimize your PDF documents.

Organize PDF

Merge PDF

To merge two or more PDF files, upload your documents from your device. You can arrange the files in your preferred order before combining them into one PDF.

After uploading your files, click on the Merge PDF button. Your documents will be processed and you can download the combined PDF instantly.

PDFMaster supports multiple PDF files and provides a fast way to combine documents without losing quality.

Split PDF

To split a PDF into multiple files, upload your PDF document and select the pages you want to separate.

You can split pages by selecting specific page ranges or extract individual pages from your document. After processing, download your newly created PDF files.

Remove PDF Pages

To remove unwanted pages from a PDF, upload your file and view the page preview. Select the pages you want to delete and click the Remove Pages button.

Your updated PDF will be generated without the selected pages.

Organize PDF Pages

PDFMaster allows you to rearrange PDF pages easily. Upload your document and drag and drop pages to change their order.

You can also delete unnecessary pages and create a perfectly organized PDF file.

Optimize PDF

Compress PDF

To reduce PDF file size, upload your document and choose your preferred compression level.

  • High Compression - Smaller file size
  • Balanced Compression - Recommended quality
  • Low Compression - Better quality

Click Compress PDF and download your optimized document. Compressed PDFs are easier to share through email and online platforms.

Edit PDF

Edit PDF Documents

To edit your PDF file, upload your document into the PDF editor.

You can add text, images, shapes, and annotations to your pages. You can also resize, move, and customize elements according to your needs.

Add Page Numbers

To add page numbers to your PDF, upload your document and choose the page number position, style, and format.

Click Add Page Numbers and download your updated PDF file.

Add Watermark

To protect your documents, you can add a watermark using text or images.

Upload your PDF, customize watermark position, transparency, and style, then click Add Watermark.

Convert Files To PDF

JPG to PDF

Convert your images into professional PDF documents. Upload JPG images, arrange them in order, select page settings, and click Convert to PDF.

Word to PDF

Convert Microsoft Word documents into PDF format while maintaining your original text formatting and layout.

Upload your DOC or DOCX file and click Convert to PDF.

PowerPoint to PDF

Transform PowerPoint presentations into PDF files. All slides, images, and text will be converted into a high-quality PDF document.

Excel to PDF

Convert Excel spreadsheets into PDF format while keeping tables and formatting intact.

HTML to PDF

Convert webpages into PDF documents. Enter your webpage URL, customize page settings, and export your webpage as a PDF.

Convert PDF To Other Formats

PDF to JPG

Convert PDF pages into JPG images. You can extract individual images or convert complete PDF pages.

PDF to Word

Convert PDF files into editable Word documents. Your text and formatting will be preserved as much as possible.

PDF to PowerPoint

Convert PDF presentations into editable PowerPoint slides.

PDF to Excel

Extract tables and data from PDF documents and convert them into Excel files.

PDF Security

Protect PDF

Secure your PDF files by adding password protection. Upload your document, create a password, and protect your sensitive information.

Unlock PDF

Remove password restrictions from PDF files that you have permission to access. Upload your PDF and use the Unlock PDF tool.

Sign PDF

Add digital signatures to your PDF documents. You can draw your signature, upload an image, or add a text-based signature.

Technical Information

Password Protected Files

PDFMaster processes your documents securely. Passwords used for protected files are not stored after processing.

File Processing

Processing speed depends on your internet connection and file size. Large files may require additional processing time.

Downloading Files

After processing is complete, you can download your converted PDF file instantly. Processed files may be automatically removed from servers after a limited time for security reasons.

Supported Browsers

PDFMaster works best with modern browsers including:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Safari

Need Help?

If you experience any problem while using PDFMaster tools, please contact our support team. We are always working to provide a simple, fast, and secure PDF experience.
