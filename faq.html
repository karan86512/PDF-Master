Common Questions

What is PDFMaster?

PDFMaster is an online PDF platform that helps users merge, convert, compress, edit, and manage PDF documents easily.

Is PDFMaster free to use?

Yes, PDFMaster provides free PDF tools with simple and easy-to-use features.

Are my PDF files secure?

Yes, user privacy and document security are our top priorities. Files are processed securely.

Do I need to install software?

No. PDFMaster works directly in your browser without installing additional software.

Can I use PDFMaster on mobile?

Yes, PDFMaster is designed to work smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

How fast are PDF conversions?

Most PDF tasks are completed quickly depending on file size and internet speed.
