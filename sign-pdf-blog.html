Why Sign PDF Documents?

Many business and personal documents require signatures before they become official. Contracts, agreements, invoices, application forms, and legal paperwork can all be signed digitally, saving time and eliminating the need to print, sign, and scan documents.

With PDFMaster, you can securely sign PDF files online from any device. Whether you're working from home, in the office, or on the go, adding a digital signature is quick, simple, and convenient.

How to Sign a PDF

1

Upload Your PDF

Click the Choose PDF File button and upload the document you want to sign.

2

Create or Upload Your Signature

Draw your signature with your mouse or finger, type your name, or upload an image of your handwritten signature.

3

Place the Signature

Drag and position your signature anywhere on the PDF document where it is required.

4

Save the Document

Click the Sign PDF button to apply your signature securely.

5

Download the Signed PDF

Download your signed PDF instantly and share it via email or cloud storage.
