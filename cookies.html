Cookies Policy

At PDFMaster, we use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, provide better services, analyze website performance, and make our platform more secure. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your cookie preferences when using our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. These files help websites remember information about your visit, such as your preferences, settings, and activities.

Cookies allow websites to work more efficiently and provide users with a smoother and more personalized experience. They do not damage your device and generally cannot access personal files stored on your device.

2. How We Use Cookies

PDFMaster uses cookies for several important purposes, including:

  • Website Functionality: Cookies help our website operate properly and allow users to access different features and tools.
  • User Preferences: Cookies remember your settings, such as theme preferences, language choices, and previous actions.
  • Performance Improvement: We use cookies to understand how visitors use our website and improve loading speed, design, and overall user experience.
  • Security: Cookies help us detect suspicious activities and protect our website from unauthorized access.
  • Analytics: Cookies help us collect anonymous information about website traffic, visitor behavior, and popular features.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Different types of cookies may be used on PDFMaster depending on their purpose.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the basic operation of our website. Without these cookies, some features and services may not function correctly.

Performance Cookies

Performance cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They provide information such as which pages are visited most often and whether users experience any errors.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow our website to remember your choices and provide enhanced features. For example, they may remember your preferred settings or interface preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us analyze website performance and improve our services based on user interaction data. The information collected is generally anonymous.

Advertising Cookies

If advertising services are used on our website in the future, advertising cookies may be used to display relevant advertisements and measure advertising performance.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated with PDFMaster may place cookies on your device. These third parties may include analytics providers, security services, or advertising partners.

We do not control cookies placed by third-party services. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party providers to understand how they use cookies and collect information.

5. How Long Do Cookies Stay on Your Device?

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods depending on their type.

  • Session Cookies: These cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These cookies remain on your device until they expire or you manually delete them.

6. Managing Cookies

You have the option to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow users to accept, reject, or delete cookies.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and performance of some parts of PDFMaster.

7. Browser Cookie Settings

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy

8. Cookies and Personal Information

Cookies themselves usually do not directly identify you. However, information collected through cookies may be combined with other information to provide better services and improve website security.

We respect your privacy and handle collected information according to our Privacy Policy.

9. Updates to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our services.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage users to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Cookies Policy, you can contact us through our website support channels.
