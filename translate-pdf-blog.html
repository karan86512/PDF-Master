How to Translate a PDF

1

Upload Your PDF

Click the Choose PDF File button and upload the document you want to translate.

2

Select the Original Language

Choose the language of your PDF or allow the system to detect it automatically if supported.

3

Select the Target Language

Choose the language into which you want to translate your document.

4

Translate PDF

Click the Translate PDF button. PDFMaster processes the document while maintaining its layout as much as possible.

5

Download the Translated PDF

Download your translated document instantly and share it anywhere.
