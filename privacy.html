Privacy Policy

Welcome to PDFMaster. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information and maintaining the confidentiality of your documents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage information when you use our website and online PDF tools. By accessing or using PDFMaster, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

PDFMaster provides a variety of online PDF services including merging, splitting, compressing, converting, editing, signing, organizing, protecting, unlocking, and other document-related tools. While delivering these services, we strive to process your files securely and responsibly while respecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect different types of information to provide and improve our services.

Information You Provide

  • Name (if submitted through contact forms)
  • Email address
  • Support requests
  • Feedback and messages
  • Uploaded PDF documents for processing

Automatically Collected Information

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Pages visited
  • Session duration
  • Error logs

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used only for legitimate business purposes and to provide a better user experience.

  • Provide PDF processing services.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Detect technical issues.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse.
  • Improve user experience.
  • Develop new features.

We do not use your uploaded documents for advertising purposes or to build user profiles unrelated to providing our services.

Document Security

Protecting your files is one of our highest priorities. Documents uploaded to PDFMaster are processed through secure systems designed to help protect your information during upload, processing, and download.

We use encrypted HTTPS connections to reduce the risk of unauthorized interception while files are transferred between your device and our servers.

Uploaded files are handled only for the purpose of completing your requested PDF operation. Temporary processing storage may be used where necessary to complete your request.

Cookies

PDFMaster uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, remember user preferences, analyze traffic, and enhance performance.

Cookies may be used for:

  • Remembering preferences
  • Keeping sessions active
  • Website analytics
  • Performance optimization
  • Security monitoring

Most browsers allow you to disable or remove cookies through browser settings. Some website features may not function correctly if cookies are disabled.

Third-Party Services

PDFMaster may use carefully selected third-party providers for hosting, analytics, payment processing, customer support, and infrastructure management. These providers process information only as required to deliver their services and according to their own privacy obligations.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party services they interact with while using our platform.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request corrections where appropriate.
  • Request deletion of eligible information.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns.

Data Protection

We continuously review our security practices and administrative safeguards to maintain a secure environment for document processing. While no online service can guarantee absolute security, PDFMaster works to reduce risks through responsible operational practices and ongoing improvements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements, legal requirements, operational changes, or new features. Updated versions become effective once published on this page. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to stay informed.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the PDFMaster support team through our Contact page.

Thank you for trusting PDFMaster. We remain committed to providing secure, reliable, and privacy-focused PDF tools for users around the world.
